Privacy Policy

Focus Timer · Last updated: 2 November 2026

Short version: Focus Timer does not collect, transmit, or share any data. Everything stays on your phone.

What the app stores

The app saves your settings and session state on your own iPhone only: your timer presets, checkpoint sound choice, wallpaper photos (if you add any), goals, reminders, and the session you currently have running or paused. This information never leaves your phone. We cannot see it. It is removed when you delete the app.

Your photos, videos, and sound files

If you choose your own wallpaper photos, or import a sound from an audio or video file, the app copies that file into its own local storage on your phone so it can use it as a background or checkpoint sound. These files are never uploaded anywhere — they stay on your device, the same as any other app setting.

Analytics and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking of any kind, and makes no network requests at all.

What the app does not do

  • It has no account or sign-in.
  • It contains no advertising.
  • It does not collect or transmit any data, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

Focus Timer is free, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders or checkpoint alerts, the app asks your permission to show notifications. These are scheduled by your iPhone itself. No server is involved. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in iPhone Settings.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes
