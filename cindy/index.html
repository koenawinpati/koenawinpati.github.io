Cindy Challenge

Your iPhone camera auto-counts pull-ups, push-ups, and squats for the classic 20-minute CrossFit benchmark workout "Cindy" — no wearables, no tapping, no guessing your round count.

Common questions

How does the rep counting work?

Prop your phone up facing you, and the app uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to watch your form and automatically count pull-ups, push-ups, and squats as you move. A tracking indicator on screen confirms when it has a clear view of you before you start.

Can I try it before subscribing?

Yes. Practice mode lets you try real camera rep-counting on any of the three exercises, with no timer and no pressure, completely free.

What does the subscription unlock?

Camera auto-counting for the full timed 20-minute Cindy workout, your round history and progress chart, and the optional Game Center leaderboard. Two plans: Monthly ($4.99, includes a 3-day free trial) and Annual ($29.99). Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple ID account settings.

Is my video recorded or uploaded anywhere?

No. All camera processing happens entirely on your device. Nothing from your camera is ever recorded, saved, or sent anywhere — see the Privacy Policy for details.

Why does round counting seem off?

For the clearest tracking, make sure your whole body is in frame, the area is well lit, and there's a plain surface behind you. The on-screen tracking indicator turns green once your pose is being picked up reliably — start your round only after it does.

Is Game Center required?

No. Connecting Game Center to submit scores to the leaderboard is entirely optional and never required to use the app.

What iPhone and iOS version do I need?

iOS 17 or later, iPhone only.
