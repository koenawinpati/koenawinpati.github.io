Focus Timer

A calm, free timer for meditation, studying, and focused work. Set your time, and a gentle bell checks in with you at each interval — no clock-watching required.

Common questions

What timer modes are there?

Intervals for a simple total-length-and-bell-interval session, Work / rest for alternating focus and break blocks, Custom for a specific sequence of blocks you define, and Builder for assembling your own session piece by piece.

Can I change the checkpoint sound?

Yes. Choose from the built-in single/double/triple beep, import your own audio file, or import the audio track straight from a video — all under Settings. You can always reset back to the default.

Can I use my own photos as the background?

Yes. Add one or more of your own photos under Settings to replace the default animated background with a slideshow, and set how long each photo stays on screen. Reset to default brings back the original animation at any time.

What happens if I close the app or restart my phone mid-session?

Focus Timer picks up where you left off, using your phone's own clock to catch up on any time that passed while it wasn't open.

How do reminders and goals work?

The Reminders tab lets you schedule notifications to start a session. The Goals tab tracks a weekly target and credits it automatically when a session completes.

Does the app cost anything?

No. Focus Timer is free, with no account, no ads, and no in-app purchases.
