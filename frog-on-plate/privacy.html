Privacy Policy

Frog on Plate · Last updated: 12 November 2026

Short version: your tasks stay on your iPhone. Frog on Plate has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no task-data server.

What the app stores

The app saves your to-dos, reminders, planning history, settings (appearance, colour tones, text size, vibration) and onboarding answers in a database on your iPhone. This is never sent to us. Your own iCloud or device backup may include it, and deleting the app removes it.

Widget

If you add the home-screen widget, the app shares a snapshot of your to-dos with it through a private container on your iPhone (an iOS "app group"). We never receive it.

Notifications

Reminders and planning nudges are scheduled and shown by your iPhone itself. No server is involved.

Purchases

Apple processes payments through your Apple Account. The app checks Apple-verified transactions on your device to unlock Premium. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy covers that information.

What the app does not do

  • It has no account or sign-in.
  • It contains no advertising.
  • It uses no analytics and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes
