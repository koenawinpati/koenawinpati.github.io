Privacy Policy

Koenawin Pati (ကိုးနဝင်း ပုတီး) · Last updated: 11 October 2026

Short version: your practice stays on your phone. The only thing we ever send anywhere is an optional, one-time, anonymous question about your age and gender — and only if you choose to answer it.

What the app stores

The app saves your practice on your own iPhone only: the date you started, which days you have completed, how many beads you have counted today, and your settings (language, light or dark, vibration and sound, reminder times, and the words of your own chant if you write one).

This information never leaves your phone. We cannot see it. It is removed when you delete the app.

Analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics (provided by Google) to understand, in aggregate, who's using the app — not to identify you personally. This covers two things: a one-time question asking your gender and age bracket, shown once and skippable, sent anonymously and never linked to your name or your practice data; and the standard, anonymous usage information Firebase automatically collects for any app that uses it, not tied to your identity and not used to track you across other apps or websites. None of this is linked to your practice data, which stays on your phone exactly as described above.

What the app does not do

  • It has no account or sign-in.
  • It contains no advertising.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

If you subscribe, your purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details — no card number, no billing address. Apple's own privacy policy covers that information.

Notifications

If you turn on a reminder, the app asks your permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled by your iPhone itself. No server is involved. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in iPhone Settings.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions? Write to smellofrainb4rain@gmail.com.
