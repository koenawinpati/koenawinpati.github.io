Frog on Plate

A calm iPhone planner for to-dos and reminders. Plan tomorrow tonight, then tackle your most important task (your "frog") first thing.

Common questions

How do I add a to-do or reminder?

Tap the ＋ next to the title on the To-Dos or Reminders list. Tap an existing row to edit it.

How do I complete or delete something?

Swipe a row right to complete it (or dismiss a reminder); swipe left to delete it. A short Undo message appears after deleting.

Can I put my list in my own order?

Touch and hold a row, then drag it. This switches the list to "My order".

How does evening planning work?

Pick a planning time in Settings › Daily planning. At that time a reminder nudges you to set up tomorrow's to-dos, so the day starts with a plan.

Does it have a home screen widget?

Premium includes home-screen widgets. Add the Frog on Plate widget from your iPhone's home screen (Edit › Add Widget › search "Frog on Plate").

Can I change the colours or text size?

Open Settings. Appearance can follow your phone, or be set to Light or Dark, each with its own colour tone. Text size has 5 steps, on top of your iPhone's own setting.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no task-data server. See the Privacy Policy.

Does the app cost anything?

To-dos, reminders, tomorrow planning and one evening reminder are free. Premium adds follow-up nudges, Logs and home-screen widgets, as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time Lifetime purchase. Current prices are shown in the app before you confirm with Apple.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings › Premium › Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Lifetime is a one-time purchase with nothing to cancel.
