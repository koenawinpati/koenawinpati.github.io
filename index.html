Common questions

When does the practice start?

Koe Nawin always begins on a Monday. If you start on another day, the app waits for the coming Monday. If you already began before installing, tap "Already started?" on the first screen and choose your day.

What if I miss a day?

As in the traditional practice, each day's rounds must be finished within that day. If a day is missed, the practice starts again from Day 1.

How do I switch language or dark mode?

Open the Settings tab. Language can be Burmese or English. Appearance can follow your phone, or be Light or Dark.

How do reminders work?

In Settings you can turn on a daily reminder, and a separate reminder for vegetarian days (the 5th day of each level), each with its own time. Allow notifications when your iPhone asks. You can turn them off at any time.
